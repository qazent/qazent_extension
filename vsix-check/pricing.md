# QAZent PR Assistant – Pricing

QAZent PR Assistant is offered under a simple, flat-rate Bring-Your-Own-License (BYOL) model from **QAZent.com**. Billing and licensing are handled directly by Lemon Squeezy (owned by Stripe). Please see https://qazent.com/support for more info regarding licensing, billing and activation. 

## 14-Day Free Trial
All new organizations receive a **14-day free trial** with full functionality. No credit card is required during the trial period.

## Subscription Pricing
QAZent's PR Assistant is available at a single, transparent price:

### **€9.99 per month (flat rate)**
- Unlimited users within your Azure DevOps organization
- Unlimited PR analyses
- AI-generated test cases
- Full Azure DevOps integration
- Access to all features
- Support included

There are no per-user fees, no usage-based billing, and no hidden costs.

## Billing Model
Licenses are billed monthly. Annual billing options are available upon request.

## Support
All paid subscriptions include support as described in our support policy.  
Support portal: **https://www.qazent.com/support**

## Licensing Model
This extension uses the **BYOL (Bring Your Own License)** model. Your Azure DevOps organization must maintain an active license to continue using the extension after the trial period.

For questions about licensing or invoicing, contact **oskar@qazent.com**.
